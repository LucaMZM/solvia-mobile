The package lock lists `node-forge` as `BSD-3-Clause OR GPL-2.0`, so the
permissive alternative is available for that transitive tooling package.
`qrcode-terminal` and `requireg` have no licence field in this lockfile, but
their installed packages include Apache 2.0 and MIT licence files,
respectively. No strong copyleft requirement was identified
in the direct runtime dependency declarations, but this is not a substitute
for a full legal review of redistributed binaries.

Unused Expo starter fonts and image placeholders, including unverified legacy branding, are excluded from this public copy. Before commercial app distribution, confirm the Open-Meteo plan and attribution placement in every weather view.
